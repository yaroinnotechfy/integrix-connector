What is IntegriX (Odoo)

IntegriX is an AI-powered platform that helps organizations digitize and streamline inspection, defect management, and remediation workflows across operations, maintenance, contractors, and field teams.

IntegriX helps eliminate:

  • Limited visibility into asset condition and operational issues that can lead to delays, quality problems, or unplanned downtime.
  • Manual and disconnected inspection processes managed through paper forms, spreadsheets, emails, and messaging tools.
  • Inefficient coordination between internal teams, contractors, vendors, and clients during remediation workflows.

The platform delivers real value through:

  • Real-time dashboards and activity feed — full visibility into ongoing inspections and task status at any moment.
  • AI-powered assistance that improves defect capture, workflow efficiency, and remediation management.
  • Secure centralized collaboration where internal teams, contractors, and vendors coordinate within a single controlled environment.
  • Web and mobile offline-ready experience — field teams can work without connectivity and sync automatically.
  • ERP integration — seamless connection with Odoo for end-to-end operational visibility.
